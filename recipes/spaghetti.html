Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti is a popular Italian pasta dish made with long, thin noodles and a variety of sauces. It is a versatile meal that can be enjoyed with different ingredients and flavors.

This recipe features a classic meat sauce made with ground beef and marinara sauce, perfect for a comforting dinner.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces spaghetti noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add spaghetti noodles and cook according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a skillet, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add minced garlic to the skillet and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the marinara sauce and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
  5. Combine the cooked spaghetti with the meat sauce, mixing well.
  6. Serve hot, topped with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley if desired.
  7. Enjoy your delicious spaghetti!
