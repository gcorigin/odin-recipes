Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family gatherings or special occasions.

This recipe combines traditional ingredients with a simple preparation method to create a delicious and satisfying dish. Serve it with a side of garlic bread and a fresh salad for a complete meal.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, egg, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  7. Layer 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce, followed by half of the ricotta mixture, half of the meat sauce, and half of the mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers, finishing with noodles topped with remaining meat sauce and mozzarella cheese.
  9. Sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top.
  10. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden.
  11. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.
  12. Enjoy your delicious homemade lasagna!
