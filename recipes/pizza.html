Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a round, flat base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients (such as anchovies, olives, vegetables, meat, etc.), which is then baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven.

This recipe provides a simple way to make delicious homemade pizza with your favorite toppings. Perfect for a family dinner or a fun gathering with friends!

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup pepperoni slices
  • 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup sliced onions
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Transfer the rolled dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top of the sauce.
  6. Add pepperoni slices and any additional toppings you desire.
  7. Sprinkle dried oregano, salt, and pepper over the toppings.
  8. Bake in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  9. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing.
  10. Enjoy your homemade pizza!
